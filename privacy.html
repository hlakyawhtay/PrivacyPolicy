Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for The Key VPN

This privacy policy explains how The Key VPN collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the mobile app and VPN service.

Last updated: 2026-06-03

Information We Collect

We collect only the data needed to provide, secure, support, and improve The Key VPN. This may include:

  • Account information, such as your user ID, email address, display name, and authentication provider.
  • Device information, such as operating system, app version, platform, generated device installation ID, and device name.
  • VPN service information, such as selected server, connection state, connection timestamps, diagnostics, and technical information needed to route VPN traffic.
  • Plan and payment support information, such as selected plan, payment proof image or file, request status, and related admin review details.
  • Notification information, such as Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens used to send important service updates.
  • App usage, crash, and performance information from the app and third-party services listed below.

VPN Service and Android VpnService

The Key VPN uses Android VpnService to create a secure device-level VPN tunnel when you choose to connect. While connected, internet traffic from apps on your device is routed through the selected VPN server so the VPN service can provide privacy, security, and connectivity functionality.

The VPN tunnel uses Outline-compatible encrypted transport between your device and the VPN endpoint. We do not use VpnService to redirect ads, manipulate traffic for advertising or monetization, sell traffic data, or collect personal and sensitive data for purposes unrelated to providing the VPN service.

The app does not use VpnService to collect or store your browsing history. VPN servers may process technical network information transiently as needed to route your traffic and maintain the service. Any server-side logs, diagnostics, or security records are used only to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and enforce the service.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to operate the app, provide the VPN service, improve performance, and deliver a better user experience.

  • Authenticate users and manage account access.
  • Register and validate devices for service access.
  • Provide VPN connections, show connection status, and troubleshoot connection issues.
  • Process plan requests, payment proof, approvals, rejections, and support requests.
  • Send important service notifications and respond to user requests.
  • Analyze app performance, detect abuse, fix bugs, and protect the service.

App Permissions

The Key VPN requests permissions only when they are needed for core functionality.

Permissions used in this app include:

  • Network access permissions used to connect to the internet and VPN servers.
  • Android VpnService permission used to create the VPN tunnel after you grant VPN consent through Android.
  • Foreground service permission used to keep the VPN connection active and show VPN status while connected.
  • Notification permission used to show VPN status and send important account or service updates.
  • Device information access used to identify and validate devices for your account.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help deliver the app and analyze performance.

Examples include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Storage
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data or VPN traffic data. Data may be shared only when necessary:

  • With service providers who support app operation, authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, payment review, and VPN service delivery.
  • To comply with legal requirements.
  • To protect rights, safety, or property.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access or disclosure. VPN traffic is encrypted from your device to the VPN tunnel endpoint.

While no system is entirely secure, we maintain industry-standard measures to protect account, device, plan, payment support, and service data.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the app or comply with legal requirements.

Retention details:

  • Account, device, plan, payment proof, notification, and support records are retained while your account is active and for a reasonable time afterward as needed for service operation, fraud prevention, support, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.
  • VPN connection diagnostics and technical server records are retained only as long as needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and enforce the service.

To delete your account or personal data, email info@thekey.com and request deletion. Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Children's Privacy

The Key VPN is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will revise the effective date at the top. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
